MEASURE AT HOME

THREE EASY STEPS.

  1. 01

    Place a sheet of paper against a wall. Stand on it with your heel lightly touching the wall.

  2. 02

    Mark the end of your longest toe. Measure from the paper edge to the mark.

  3. 03

    Measure both feet and use the longer measurement. Compare it with the chart.

PRO TIP / Measure near the end of the day, wearing the socks you use at work.
