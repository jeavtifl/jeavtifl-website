GOOD TO KNOW.

Our styles use standard U.S. sizing. If you are between sizes or prefer extra room, choose the next half size up. See our complete size guide before ordering.

JEAVTIFL footwear is available through Amazon. Every product page links directly to the relevant Amazon listing or JEAVTIFL results page.

No footwear can prevent every slip. JEAVTIFL outsoles are designed to improve traction on common workplace surfaces. Always follow workplace safety procedures and promptly clean spills.

Remove loose debris, wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap, then air dry away from direct heat. Do not machine wash unless your product care label specifically allows it.

Orders are fulfilled by Amazon and subject to the return policy displayed on your order and product listing. Visit Your Orders on Amazon to begin a return.
