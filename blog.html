FEATURED GUIDE

COMFORT IS A SYSTEM.

Long shifts challenge your whole body. Small, consistent habits can help you feel fresher from prep through close.

01 / START WITH FIT

Your heel should feel secure, your toes should have room to move, and the widest part of your foot should align with the widest part of the shoe. Measure both feet and fit the larger one.

02 / ROTATE AND RESET

When possible, rotate between two pairs so cushioning can recover and moisture can dry. Remove insoles after especially demanding shifts and let shoes air dry.

03 / MOVE WITH INTENTION

Change position regularly, take permitted breaks, and use anti-fatigue mats where your workplace provides them. Follow your employer's safety guidance at all times.
